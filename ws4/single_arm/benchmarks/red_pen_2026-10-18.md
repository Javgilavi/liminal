# Red-pen benchmark on the Haller, 18 Oct 2026 (05:00-05:35 CEST)

Task "put the red pen in its slot in the cardboard box", robot-left (task) arm, steps reach / grasp / insert, scored
by Oscar after each attempt. One `ws4/eval/trials.jsonl` line per attempt; per-attempt rows (run folder, end reason,
keep-out refusals, chunks) in [red_pen_2026-10-18.json](red_pen_2026-10-18.json). Regenerate with
`python ws4/single_arm/benchmark.py`.

**Headline:** FLUX RAW 5k (B84 + `lora_pen84` checkpoint 005000) at an action horizon of 16 gave the first full
real-arm inserts: 3 of 5 attempts (layouts A, B, C), 9 of 15 steps. ACT pen84 100k reached and grasped in 4 of 5
but never inserted (8 of 15).

## Setup (same for every pen84-table block)

- Table rebuilt as the pen84 training scene, layouts A-D placed on the placement page before each attempt; lid open on
  the box (lying flat at 04:45, standing from about 05:00; see each attempt's `start_check.jpg`).
- Station moved back toward the pen84 recording spot with `align_station.py`: wrist room view offset -11 px, scale
  0.97. Room darker than in the recordings: wrist brightness 0.34-0.6 of the reference, falling during the session.
- Box arm folded, torque off, at [3.9, -89.3, 98.7, 63.9, 14.6] (wrist flex 64 vs 78 in the takes): other-arm
  tolerance 15 deg (Oscar's override, as for ACT).
- Runner: 1 deg/tick guard, LeRobot cap 20 deg, 60 % torque cap, FK keep-out with the pen84 demo envelope, load
  freeze, stall freeze (8 deg for 15 ticks); 120 s per attempt; FLUX served on real-serve-6000 (guidance 1, compile),
  chunk 42.
- Changes per block are in the table: action horizon (commands executed per chunk; 32 is the package default) and
  the shoulder-lift stall threshold (25 deg instead of 8 for the lift only).

## What each change did

1. **Scene.** With the lid held up by the box arm (a scene the pen84 LoRA never saw) FLUX held still at home and
   drifted: 0 of 15 steps. Offline, live frames gave hold chunks while recorded frames gave the reach; the wrist
   camera's view of the room decided it (handover "Update 06:20"-"08:50"). On the rebuilt pen84 table every block
   starts moving within 3 s and closes the gripper in about 1.5-3 s, like the demos.
2. **Checkpoint (horizon 32, stall 8).** RAW 5k 8/15 > EMA 10k 4/15 > RAW 10k 3/15; none inserted. The stall freeze
   ended 9 of these 15 attempts: the shoulder lift trailed its command by 8-10 deg in the air at about two thirds of
   the 60 % torque cap (no contact).
3. **Lift stall 25 (horizon 32).** One attempt: the load freeze stopped it instead (lift at 72 % of the cap). The
   lift is torque-limited at the cap; the stall threshold alone does not help.
4. **Horizon 16 (lift stall 25).** 3 of 5 full inserts (A 94 s, B 91 s, C 48 s). Replanning every 16 commands (about
   0.5 s) with the same chunks; the arm still waits about 0.6 s for each chunk.
5. **Horizon 8 (lift stall 25).** 0 of 2 (A, B), both stopped by Oscar after 66-70 s without a reach; the arm stands
   still about 70 % of the time, and the wrist view was at its darkest (0.34).

## Caveats

- Small blocks (5, or fewer for horizon 8 and the 25-deg-only block); layouts as listed in the notes.
- Horizon 16 and 8 also had the 25-deg lift stall threshold: two changes against the horizon-32 blocks.
- Interventions are as scored. The three successes and every horizon-8/16 failure ended with Oscar's stop key (the
  `s` key would have logged "operator: success"); stops after the insert are not interventions in the task sense.
- Lighting fell through the session; the horizon-8 block ran in the darkest conditions.
- Other-arm tolerance 15 deg (box arm wrist flex 64 instead of 78) for every pen84-table block, ACT's included.

## Code changes made during the session (ws4/single_arm)

- `align_station.py`: live station alignment against a recording's home view (cameras only).
- `run_single_arm.py`: `--action-steps` (env `WS4_ACTION_STEPS`), `--lift-stall-deg` (env `WS4_LIFT_STALL_DEG`),
  per-joint ramp arrival tolerance (lift 8 deg), and a joint-by-joint ramp fallback when the keep-out refuses the
  straight ramp (after an insert the return ramp hit the torso zone). Tests: `tests/test_single_arm.py`.
- `benchmark.py`: this table.
