The hardest bug

Re-planning every 16 actions made it work

Symptom

FLUX reached and grasped, then stalled before the insert, or held still at home.

Diagnosis

Its decision hangs on what it sees: a 1 px shift of the wrist view flipped hold and reach.

Fix

Execute 16 of each chunk’s actions instead of 32: FLUX re-plans every 0.5 s on fresh frames. 3/5 inserts, 0/5 at 32.
