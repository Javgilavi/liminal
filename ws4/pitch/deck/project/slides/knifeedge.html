The hardest bug

One pixel of wrist view flips hold and reach

Symptom

After the robot moved, FLUX held still at home on live frames, and reached on recorded ones.

Diagnosis

A field-by-field request diff: only the wrist image differed. A 1 px shift or 1 deg tilt flipped it.

Fix

Align the station to the recording view before every run; train with crop and colour augmentation.

K1 THE KNIFE EDGE (110 to 140 s). "The hardest bug: after the robot moved, FLUX just held still. A field-by-field diff of live versus recorded requests found one difference, the wrist camera's view of the room. One pixel of shift flipped hold to reach. So we built a station-alignment tool and train with image augmentation." Evidence (offline, saved live frames): live and recorded requests identical in task string, image keys and order, shapes, dtypes, units and history. Recorded images with live state and commands: reach (elbow +26.7 deg, gripper -96.5 %); live images with recorded state: hold. Sweep: 0 of 27 wrist shifts and 0 of 10 seeds reach on the live view; averaging never rescues it. Brightness matters too: wrist gain x1.6 to 2.0 made the gripper close. The benchmark ran after align_station.py brought the wrist view to -11 px and scale 0.97 of the recording. Augmented LoRAs and their pre-robot gate (from rest the first chunk must move >= 20 deg on every held-out take, also under 2 px shifts and half brightness): cutter 44.2 deg pass (served), green 73.3 pass, box 4.9 to 5.5 fail for every variant (skills.md).
